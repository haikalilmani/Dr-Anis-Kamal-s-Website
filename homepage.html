About Dr. Syakira

Hi, I am Dr. Syakira Kamal, a dedicated Consultant Obstetrician & Gynecologist. My practice is built on the core tenets of empathy, safety, and patient autonomy. By providing clear understanding of your circumstances and potential outcomes, I can empower you to make the best choices for you and your baby.

With over 10 years of experience in high-risk obstetrics and minimally invasive gynecology, I strive to provide a safe space where your concerns are heard and addressed with professional excellence.

Credentials

  • MRCOG (UK)
  • Fellowship in Maternal Medicine and Urogynecology
